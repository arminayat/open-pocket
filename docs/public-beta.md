# Open Pocket for iPhone and Mac

An independent, unofficial app for owners of an already-activated Pocket recorder.

**iPhone:** connect your recorder, download recordings over Bluetooth, play and share audio, and write notes. Optional features include transcription with your own provider API key, AI titles and summaries, and iCloud Drive sync. API providers may charge for usage.

**Mac:** use your Pocket as the microphone for the dictation app you already use, such as OpenWhispr, Wispr Flow, or Superwhisper, and tap the recorder to trigger shortcuts, apps, and media keys. Requires macOS 14 or later and the included Pocket Mic audio driver, which you install by dragging it into place.

You need a compatible physical Pocket recorder, activated using its manufacturer's app, to obtain recordings. Open Pocket does not activate recorders or modify their firmware. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the maker of Pocket.
