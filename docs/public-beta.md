# OpenPocket for iPhone

An independent, unofficial client for owners of an already-activated Pocket recorder.

Connect your recorder, download recordings over Bluetooth, play and share audio, and write notes. Optional features include transcription with your own provider API key, AI titles and summaries, and iCloud Drive sync. API providers may charge for usage.

You need a compatible physical Pocket recorder, activated using its manufacturer's app, to obtain recordings. OpenPocket does not activate recorders or modify their firmware. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the maker of Pocket.

## TestFlight beta

[Join the beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/qkq1GB2v). External installation requires Apple's approval of a build; the link may not accept testers while review is pending. This is experimental beta software, not an App Store release. Keep original recordings and only record or upload content you are authorized to process.
